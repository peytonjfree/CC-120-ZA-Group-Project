Creepy Crawly Cave

Step into the dark and use a blacklight to make our scorpions and spiders glow.

The big idea

In the cave, scorpions and spiders look dull in normal light. Switch on the blacklight and a scorpion lights up in glowing blue-green. Try it here, then try it for real in the cave.

Who lives in the cave

Scorpions

They have pincers and a stinger on a curled tail. They hunt at night, and mothers carry their babies on their backs.

Spiders

Spiders make silk from the tip of the belly. They wrap meals, build egg sacs and some spin webs, though many hunt without one.

Bugs or not?

Insects have six legs and three body parts. Scorpions and spiders have eight legs, so they are arachnids, not insects.

Spooky questions

Why do scorpions glow?

A substance in their outer shell soaks up the invisible UV light from the blacklight and gives it back as visible blue-green light. Scientists are still learning why scorpions do this.

Do spiders glow too?

Some do, in other colors, and some barely glow at all. Look for differences between the animals in the cave.

Are they dangerous?

Our animals stay safely behind glass. In nature most are shy and use venom mainly to catch food. If you find one outside, leave it alone and ask a grown-up.

Visiting tip: Use quiet voices and do not tap the glass. Cave animals like calm.
